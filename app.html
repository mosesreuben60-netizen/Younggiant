Privacy & Policy

Data We Collect

Young Giant collects your name, email, phone number, and delivery addresses to provide our logistics services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to process bookings, assign drivers, send delivery updates, and improve our service. Your phone number may be shared with your assigned driver for delivery coordination only.

Payment Security

Wallet transactions are processed securely. Card payments are handled by Paystack, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. Young Giant does not store your card details.

Location Data

We collect delivery addresses for routing purposes. Driver GPS data is collected during active deliveries only and is not stored after delivery completion.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing younggiantev@gmail.com. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Terms of Service

By using Young Giant, you agree to use our services lawfully. Young Giant is not liable for delays caused by unforeseen circumstances including road conditions, weather, or force majeure events. Refunds are processed within 48 hours of a valid claim.

Contact

For privacy concerns or data requests, contact us at younggiantev@gmail.com or call +234 700 000 0000.
